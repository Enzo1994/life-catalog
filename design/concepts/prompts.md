# A · 纸间收藏

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a polished high fidelity iOS product design presentation for 人生图鉴, a PRIVATE personal memory catalog of meaningful physical objects, NOT a shopping, inventory, finance or gamified app. Landscape 1536x1024 composition, three large perfectly straight portrait iPhone screen panels side by side, equal 390:844 aspect ratio, no device bezels, no perspective. Screens must be large and readable, with neat small Chinese page labels above. Chinese typography precise and legible. Show same sample collection and story across screens. Screen 1 图鉴架: large title, small personal subtitle 把舍不得的日常，好好收起来, search icon, a compact 一年前的今天 recall entry, 2-column bookshelf with four tangible album covers named 旅行带回来的, 每天的小确幸, 旧物有回声, 随手收藏; album covers have small real cutout physical objects, book spines and restrained shelf ledges, counts 已收录 12 件 etc, NO progress for these free-mode albums. A small 新建系列 button. Bottom tabs 图鉴架 / 拍摄 / 发现 / 我的, camera control prominent, 发现 has tiny 稍后开放 marker, no badges or streaks. Screen 2 collection 旅行带回来的: back, share, title, 已收录 12 件, small 网格 / 时间线 segmented control, 2-column evenly aligned object cutout cards, a blue-and-white ceramic mug, ticket stub, small camera, seashell, keychain, postcard. Each has tiny name and date. Grid large enough to celebrate objects. Screen 3 object story: back, share, a LARGE isolated blue-and-white ceramic mug centered inside matching surface, segmented 原图 / 抠图 / 风格图, title 京都带回的小杯子, series chip 旅行带回来的, small date 2025.10.24, story heading 它的故事, sentence 那天下着小雨，我们在巷子里的小店躲了一整个下午。, two quiet tags 旅行 / 别人送的, subtle 编辑记录 button. No prices, no likes, no public profile metrics, no fake shopping actions, no charts. Realistic miniature objects must have material detail and be original imaginary examples, not emoji. Respect iOS safe areas and 44pt controls. All screen content inside boundaries. No unnecessary English marketing slogan. Board footer small 视觉探索 · 非最终界面.
Primary reference lock: Craft Docs digital scrapbook collage. Preserve warm paper #fff3e7 main canvas, ink #030302, white paper card surfaces, soft multi-layered shadows, tactile pastel mint #9bd8a9 and marigold #fde99b ONLY for album covers/tags, not CTA. Adapt literary headings to Chinese Songti serif at 28-30pt; UI PingFang sans at 14-17pt. 14px and 24px rounded surfaces, black pill primary buttons. A beautifully crafted intimate personal journal. Series covers look like softly worn paper-bound notebooks mounted on two discreet paper shelf ledges, tiny linen tape details limited to covers. Borrow PocketShelf book-cover grouping only. Story reads like a printed personal note with clean alignment. NO brown/terracotta/olive brand accents, NO ornamental giant serif slogan, NO excessive torn paper clutter. Board background pale cool gray to distinguish from cream phone screens. Above panels show title A · 纸间收藏 and subtitle 让物品像书页一样，慢慢留下来。
